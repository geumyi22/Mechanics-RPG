STACK RULE
- Stack instances have independent expiration timers.
- Skill activation never consumes stacks.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
- Bedrock has no native Java-style Glowing status effect. Location reveal is rendered as persistent blue target marker particles for 10s.
- Attack reduction is direct outgoing-damage scaling, not Weakness.
- Movement reduction uses the movement attribute directly.
- Action lock blocks outgoing attack damage and forces movement to zero while active.
- Healing block cancels heal events and clamps direct health increases every tick.
- Buff suppression stores active positive effects (except health_boost), removes them during the block, and restores them afterward.
