Mechanics RPG v1.4.45 -> v1.4.44 rollback notes

1) Keep a copy of the current world before replacing packs.
2) v1.4.45 does NOT change the v3 Subspace storage schema or keys.
3) To return to v1.4.44 code/UI, restore the v1.4.44 BP/RP and world pack refs together.
4) Do not delete these player dynamic properties during rollback:
   geumyi:subspace_v3_head
   geumyi:subspace_v3_stage
   geumyi:subspace_v3_journal
5) geumyi:subspace_ui_mode is new in v1.4.45 and is harmless if left behind.
6) If a journal property exists, let the restored v1.4.44 recovery run before further inventory changes.
